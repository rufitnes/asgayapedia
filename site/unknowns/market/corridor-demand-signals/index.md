**Status:** Not Started  
**Priority:** Low  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-29  
**Contributors Welcome:** Yes

## What We Don't Know

Which remittance corridors show the strongest demand signals for a permissionless alternative to legacy services?

## Why It Matters

Corridor selection affects:
- Phase 1 expansion priorities
- Partnership strategies
- Marketing focus
- Developer allocation

## Current Hypothesis

Spain→Venezuela shows strongest initial demand due to diaspora size, legacy fee pain, and regulatory-friendly sender country.

## Investigation Method

1. Analyze remittance flow data by corridor
2. Research fee levels in different corridors
3. Survey diaspora communities about remittance needs
4. Evaluate regulatory environments
5. Assess competition and market gaps

## Success Criterion

Prioritized list of 3-5 target corridors for Phase 1 expansion based on demand signals and feasibility.

## Phase 0 Trial Integration

Track sender geographic distribution and any spontaneous cross-corridor interest.

## Contributor Guidance

**Skills needed:** Market research, remittance industry analysis  
**Estimated effort:** 4-6 hours  
**How to start:** Research World Bank remittance data by corridor
