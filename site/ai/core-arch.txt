SECTION 1: THREE CORE REQUIREMENTS
==============================================

Asgaya satisfies three fundamental requirements:

1. CHEAPER THAN LEGACY (<1% fees)
   - Traditional remittances: 6.49% average (World Bank)
   - Asgaya target: 1% total fee
   - Save €5.49 on every €100 transfer

2. PROMOTE BCH ADOPTION
   - Every transaction creates BCH accumulation incentives
   - Merchants earn 0.5% per transaction in BCH
   - BCH sellers earn 0.5% + hedge benefits
   - Goal: Make Asgaya redundant as BCH adoption grows

3. PERMISSIONLESS
   - No KYC (peer-to-peer coordination)
   - Minimal hardware (Android phone sufficient)
   - Minimal knowledge (if you use WhatsApp, you can use Asgaya)
   - Self-custody (users control keys via covenant signatures)

==============================================
SECTION 2: WHY CHEAPER THAN LEGACY
==============================================

PROBLEM: Traditional remittances cost 6.49% average
- Western Union: ~7% fees
- MoneyGram: ~6% fees
- Banks: 5-10% fees + poor exchange rates
- Hidden in: Service fees + exchange rate markups + receiving fees

WHY ASGAYA IS CHEAPER:
- No Kraken purchase overhead (BCH sellers provide inventory)
- No custody infrastructure (covenant smart contracts)
- No intermediation fees (peer-to-peer settlement)
- Transparent exchange rates (CoinGecko + DolarAPI, no markup)
- Free peer-to-peer rails (BCH network)

FEE BREAKDOWN (€100 transfer):
- Sender pays: €1.00 total (1%)
- BCH seller earns: €0.50 (0.5%)
- Merchant earns: €0.50 (0.5%)
- Protocol fee: €0.00 (regulatory compliance)
- Recipient receives: €100 equivalent in local cash

COMPARISON:
- Western Union €100 transfer: €6.50 fee → Recipient gets €93.50
- Asgaya €100 transfer: €1.00 fee → Recipient gets €100.00
- Savings: €5.50 per transaction (6.5× cheaper)

KEY INNOVATION: Covenant-based settlement eliminates custody overhead
while maintaining security through overcollateralization (7% buffer).

==============================================
SECTION 3: WHY PROMOTE BCH ADOPTION
==============================================

GOAL: Every transaction creates economic incentives for BCH accumulation.

BCH SELLER INCENTIVES:
- Earn 0.5% fee per transaction (€0.50 on €100)
- Hedge mechanism: Better than holding BCH in BOTH price directions
  - BCH drops 5%: Seller better off by €0.54 vs holding
  - BCH rises 5%: Seller better off by €0.46 vs holding
- 94% of value converted to fiat upfront (only ~7% exposed to volatility)

MERCHANT INCENTIVES:
- Earn 0.5% fee per transaction in BCH
- Accumulate BCH inventory through fees
- Can sell BCH locally for fiat (circular economy)
- No capital requirement (just facilitate recipient payouts)

RECIPIENT INCENTIVES:
- Direct BCH payments save 1% fee (near-zero cost vs 1% remittance fee)
- Hold BCH for future payments (avoid cash conversion)
- Participate in BCH economy (merchants accepting BCH locally)

VISION: Asgaya becomes redundant
- As BCH adoption grows, direct BCH payments replace remittances
- Senders → Recipients: Direct BCH transfer (no Asgaya needed)
- Merchants accept BCH directly (no cash conversion needed)
- Asgaya remains available for fiat on/off-ramps only

KEY PRINCIPLE: Economic incentives over ideology. Rewards drive adoption.

SECTION 4: WHY PERMISSIONLESS
==============================================

PROBLEM: Gatekeepers exclude poorest users
- KYC requirements (government ID, proof of address)
- Hardware requirements (smartphone, internet)
- Knowledge requirements (crypto terminology, wallet management)

ASGAYA APPROACH: Remove all gatekeepers

NO REGISTRATION:
- No accounts, no sign-ups, no verification
- Just install app and start using

NO IDENTITY CHECKS:
- No government ID required
- No proof of address
- No personal data collection
- Peer-to-peer coordination only

NO HARDWARE BARRIERS:
- Phase 0: Android phone (basic)
- Phase 1: RFID card (no smartphone needed)
- Future: Cardboard QR codes / feature phones

NO KNOWLEDGE BARRIERS:
- Simple UI (no crypto jargon)
- Guided flows (step-by-step)
- Auto-calculations (no manual math)

SELF-CUSTODY:
- Users control covenant signatures
- No third party can freeze funds
- Autonomous smart contract execution

KEY PRINCIPLE: Maximum freedom, minimum barriers. If you can use
WhatsApp, you can use Asgaya.

SECTION 5: WHY NO KYC
==============================================

PROBLEM: KYC excludes billions of people
- 1.4 billion unbanked globally (World Bank)
- Migrants often lack local ID
- Refugees fleeing persecution
- Rural populations without documentation

WHY ASGAYA DOESN'T NEED KYC:

1. NO CUSTODY = NO CASP LICENSE NEEDED
   - Covenant smart contracts hold funds (not Asgaya)
   - MiCA Article 3 exemption (information society service)
   - No PSD2 payment institution license required

2. PEER-TO-PEER COORDINATION
   - Asgaya provides information (like Google Search)
   - Parties coordinate directly (no intermediation)
   - No AML/KYC obligations under EU regulation

3. SMALL TRANSACTION SIZES
   - Target: €50-€500 remittances
   - Below suspicious transaction thresholds
   - Peer-to-peer cash is legal everywhere

REGULATORY COMPLIANCE:
- No custody (covenant holds funds autonomously)
- No intermediation (parties sign covenant directly)
- No protocol fee (no value extraction, information service only)
- No ability to freeze/reverse (autonomous execution)

TRADE-OFF:
- Without KYC, disputes rely on social transparency (not arbitration)
- Phase 0: No formal dispute system (trusted parties only)
- V1: Social media evidence (#AsgayaDispute, no moderation)

KEY PRINCIPLE: No custody = No KYC requirements. Design for compliance.

SECTION 6: WHY ELIMINATE VOLATILITY RISK
==============================================

PROBLEM: Bitcoin Cash price volatility creates uncertainty
- BCH can move ±5% in 24 hours
- ±10% swings in volatile markets
- Recipients need predictable value (rent, food, medicine)

SOLUTION: Pull system compresses volatility window

TRADITIONAL CRYPTO (Push System):
- Sender buys BCH → Send → Recipient waits → Sells BCH
- Volatility window: Hours to days
- Price risk: ±5-10% typical

ASGAYA (Pull System):
- Sender funds covenant → BCH seller posts overcollateralized BCH
- Recipient claims at merchant → Covenant executes in 30 seconds
- Volatility window: 30 seconds (not hours)
- Price risk: ±0.1% typical (99% reduction)

OVERCOLLATERALIZATION BUFFER:
- 7% overcollateralization (€107 BCH for €100 transfer)
- Absorbs normal volatility (daily ±3-5% moves)
- Margin call if BCH drops >7% (seller adds BCH or refund triggers)
- Split refund if timeout (merchant portion → sender, seller portion → seller)

EUR-DENOMINATED PROMISE:
- Covenant promises €100 EUR value (not BCH amount)
- Rate calculated at creation (promise rate)
- Rate calculated at maturity (execution rate)
- Recipient gets exact EUR value regardless of BCH price movement

KEY INNOVATION: Pull system + overcollateralization = volatility eliminated
for recipient, minimal exposure for BCH seller.

SECTION 7: WHY MINIMAL HARDWARE REQUIREMENTS
==============================================

PROBLEM: Hardware requirements exclude poorest users
- Smartphones cost €50-€100+
- Rural areas lack electricity for charging
- Elderly users struggle with new tech
- Children need to receive money independently

ASGAYA TIERED HARDWARE MODEL:

PHASE 0 (Current): Android Smartphone
- Minimum requirement for cryptographic confirmation
- No BCH wallet needed (vs traditional crypto)
- No private key management (covenant signatures)
- Just install app, enter codes

PHASE 1 (Planned): RFID Card
- Cost: €0.50-€2 per card
- No battery (passive NFC)
- Tap merchant's phone to confirm
- No smartphone needed

FUTURE (Vision): Cardboard QR / Feature Phone
- Cost: €0 (cardboard) or €10 (feature phone)
- SMS notifications via BCH seller bot
- Merchant-assisted completion
- Zero-hardware barrier to access

KEY PRINCIPLE: Hardware determines quality of experience, not access.
Everyone can participate at some level.

TRADE-OFF: Lower-tech options require more trust in merchant. Phase 0
uses smartphones for security, Phase 1+ explores trust models for
hardware-free recipients.

SECTION 8: WHY SELF-CUSTODY
==============================================

PROBLEM: Custodial systems create control and regulatory burdens
- Third party can freeze your funds
- Requires CASP license (MiCA)
- Requires KYC compliance
- Single point of failure

ASGAYA APPROACH: Covenant-based self-custody

HOW IT WORKS:
- Covenant smart contract holds funds (not Asgaya, not exchange)
- Parties control covenant signatures (merchant + recipient both sign)
- Autonomous execution (no human can override)
- Non-custodial (MiCA Article 3 exemption)

USER CONTROL:
- Recipients control their confirmation signature
- Merchants control their co-signature
- BCH sellers control margin call response
- No third party can unilaterally move funds

SECURITY MODEL:
- Covenant requires both signatures to execute (merchant + recipient)
- Timeout triggers split refund if no execution (merchant → sender, seller → seller)
- Overcollateralization protects against BCH seller default
- No single point of failure

REGULATORY BENEFIT:
- No custody = No CASP license needed
- No KYC requirements
- Information society service (MiCA exempt)
- Permissionless participation

KEY PRINCIPLE: Self-custody through covenant signatures. Users control
funds via cryptographic confirmation, not trusted intermediaries.

SECTION 9: DESIGN PRINCIPLES
==============================================

These principles guide all architectural decisions:

1. ACCESSIBILITY OVER PERFECTION
   - Serve users with 1% fees rather than refuse unserved markets
   - Progressive enhancement (cardboard → RFID → smartphone)
   - Design for edge case, not average case

2. ECONOMIC INCENTIVES OVER IDEOLOGY
   - Ideology alone hasn't spread BCH adoption
   - Rewards drive behavior (0.5% fees create incentives)
   - Make participation profitable, not just ideologically pure

3. SIMPLICITY AT SCALE
   - Do a few things excellently (remittances first)
   - Not many things poorly (avoid feature bloat)
   - Focus on core use case, iterate based on data

4. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE BY DESIGN
   - No custody (covenant smart contracts)
   - No intermediation (peer-to-peer coordination)
   - No protocol fee (information service only)
   - Research regulation BEFORE building (RS052 → covenant pivot)

5. TRANSPARENT ECONOMICS
   - No hidden fees
   - No exchange rate markups
   - All costs disclosed upfront
   - Open source protocol (anyone can verify)

6. EMPIRICAL VALIDATION
   - Don't hardcode arbitrary parameters without testing
   - Phase 0 validation of 12 parameters (7% overcollateralization, etc.)
   - Bitcoin block size lesson: Validate before making permanent
   - Iterate based on real transaction data

7. DOCUMENTATION FIRST
   - Validate architecture before writing code
   - Catch regulatory issues early (escrow → covenant pivot)
   - Enable external review (AI-optimized docs)
   - Build with confidence (architecture is sound)

KEY ARCHITECTURAL INNOVATIONS
==============================================

1. PULL SYSTEM
   - Recipient controls timing (not sender)
   - Compresses volatility window from hours → 30 seconds
   - Enables predictable EUR value delivery

2. OVERCOLLATERALIZED COVENANTS
   - 7% buffer absorbs BCH volatility
   - No custody (covenant holds funds autonomously)
   - MiCA/PSD2 compliant (no licensing needed)

3. BCH SELLER HEDGE MECHANISM
   - Better than holding BCH in BOTH price directions
   - 94% of value converted to fiat upfront
   - Makes protocol self-sustaining (sellers profit)

4. TWO-WAY FEE SPLIT
   - Seller 0.5%, Merchant 0.5%, Protocol 0%
   - No protocol fee = information service (regulatory compliance)
   - Both parties incentivized to participate

5. AUTONOMOUS DISPUTE RESOLUTION
   - Phase 0: No mediator (covenant executes autonomously)
   - No human can override fund distribution
   - V1: Social media transparency (#AsgayaDispute, optional)
