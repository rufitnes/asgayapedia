## Overview

Before designing Asgaya, we identified the fundamental requirements any remittance protocol must satisfy:

1. **Cost:** Must be cheaper than 5-8% traditional fees
2. **Speed:** Faster than 1-2 day Western Union transfers
3. **Accessibility:** No KYC, works for unbanked populations
4. **Compliance:** No custody = no MSB license needed
5. **Trust:** Must work between strangers (no pre-existing relationships)
6. **Stability:** Merchants must avoid volatility risk or they quit
7. **Scalability:** Must handle millions of transactions without central coordinator

---

## Core Requirements

### 1. Cross-Border Remittances Must Cost <1%
**Problem:** Western Union charges 5-8%, banks charge 8-15%  
**Why it matters:** $589B annual market, mostly low-income workers

### 2. Recipients Need Local Cash, Not Crypto
**Problem:** Crypto doesn't pay rent in Venezuela  
**Why it matters:** Merchants are the cash-out layer

### 3. No Company Should Custody Funds
**Problem:** Custody = MSB license = regulatory nightmare  
**Why it matters:** Permissionless, censorship-resistant

### 4. Must Work Without Central Coordinator
**Problem:** Central server = single point of failure  
**Why it matters:** Bulletin board on BCH blockchain

### 5. Merchants Must Avoid Volatility or They Quit
**Problem:** ±20% monthly BCH volatility kills merchant retention  
**Why it matters:** Stability layer (H€/HAu) is core feature
