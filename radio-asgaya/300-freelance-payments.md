Remember, Asgaya connects senders in Spain with recipients in Venezuela through Bitcoin Cash sellers. The seller accepts euros, provides BCH, posts the covenant. For family remittances, that's informal. An SMS notification, maybe a phone call. No paperwork.

But businesses need invoices. They need tax IDs. They need accounting-ready documentation.

That's what a Pro Seller provides. Same mechanics. Same protocol. Just with a proper business invoice that says: "Crypto Asset Purchase for Payment Covenant. One thousand five euros."
