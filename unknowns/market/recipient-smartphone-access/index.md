# Recipient Smartphone Access

**Status:** Not Started  
**Priority:** Low  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-29  
**Contributors Welcome:** Yes

## What We Don't Know

What percentage of Venezuelan remittance recipients have smartphones? What Android versions and capabilities?

## Why It Matters

Smartphone access determines:
- Direct-to-phone delivery feasibility
- SMS-only fallback requirements
- Target market size
- Feature prioritization

## Current Hypothesis

70%+ of remittance recipients in urban Venezuela have Android smartphones (version 8+), sufficient for Phase 0 targeting.

## Investigation Method

1. Research Venezuelan smartphone penetration data
2. Survey remittance recipients about device ownership
3. Analyze Android version distribution
4. Compare urban vs rural access
5. Model system reach at various penetration rates

## Success Criterion

Data-backed estimate of smartphone access in target demographic with device capability breakdown.

## Phase 0 Trial Integration

Track recipient device types and OS versions during trials.

## Contributor Guidance

**Skills needed:** Market research, survey design  
**Estimated effort:** 2-3 hours  
**How to start:** Research Venezuela smartphone penetration statistics
