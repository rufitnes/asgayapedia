# Seller Fee Sufficiency

**Status:** Not Started  
**Priority:** High  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-29  
**Contributors Welcome:** Yes

## What We Don't Know

Is the 0.5% seller fee sufficient to make BCH selling economically attractive given capital costs, risk, and operational overhead?

## Why It Matters

If 0.5% is too low, sellers won't participate and the system lacks liquidity. If it's set correctly, sellers achieve profitable capital recycling.

## Current Hypothesis

0.5% fee on high-velocity capital (360% annual turnover) provides attractive returns comparable to other DeFi opportunities.

## Investigation Method

1. Model seller economics at various capital recycling speeds
2. Compare 0.5% returns to competing investment opportunities
3. Survey potential sellers about minimum acceptable returns
4. Calculate break-even fee rate

## Success Criterion

Demonstrated seller interest at 0.5% fee level during Phase 0 trials, with measured capital recycling achieving target velocity.

## Phase 0 Trial Integration

Track seller retention, capital deployed, and recycling frequency.
