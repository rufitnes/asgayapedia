# Fiat Chargeback Risk

**Status:** Not Started  
**Priority:** Low  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-29  
**Contributors Welcome:** Yes

## What We Don't Know

How frequently do Bizum/PagoMóvil payments get reversed or charged back, and what recourse do sellers have?

## Why It Matters

If chargeback rates are high, BCH sellers face unhedged fiat risk that could make participation economically unviable.

## Current Hypothesis

Bizum/PagoMóvil instant payments are largely irreversible, with chargeback rates <0.1% (comparable to cash transactions).

## Investigation Method

1. Research Bizum/PagoMóvil chargeback policies
2. Interview merchants about chargeback experiences
3. Analyze fraud protection mechanisms
4. Model seller exposure at various chargeback rates

## Success Criterion

Documented chargeback rates and seller recourse mechanisms, with confirmed rates below 0.5%.

## Phase 0 Trial Integration

Track any chargeback incidents during trials and document resolution process.

## Contributor Guidance

**Skills needed:** Payment system research, merchant interviews  
**Estimated effort:** 2-3 hours  
**How to start:** Research Bizum terms of service and chargeback policies
