**Status:** Not Started  
**Priority:** Low  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-29  
**Contributors Welcome:** Yes

## What We Don't Know

How do merchants manage cash inventory when validating covenants? Do they maintain dedicated float, use till cash, or restock dynamically?

## Why It Matters

Merchant cash management affects:
- Maximum transaction sizes they'll handle
- Frequency of cash restocking needs
- Double-dip merchant vs single-dip merchant ratios
- System reliability (cash availability)

## Current Hypothesis

Merchants use existing till cash for small transactions (<€100 equivalent) and maintain separate float for larger amounts.

## Investigation Method

1. Interview merchants about current cash management
2. Survey about willingness to maintain dedicated float
3. Model cash flow at various transaction volumes
4. Observe actual practices in Phase 0

## Success Criterion

Documented merchant cash management strategies with measured maximum transaction sizes and restocking frequency.

## Phase 0 Trial Integration

Track transaction sizes, merchant cash availability, and any "out of cash" incidents.

**Skills needed:** Merchant operations research  
**Estimated effort:** 2-4 hours  
**How to start:** Interview small shop owners about cash handling
