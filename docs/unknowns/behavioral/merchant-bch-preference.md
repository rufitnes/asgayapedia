# Merchant BCH Preference

**Status:** Not Started  
**Priority:** Medium  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-29  
**Contributors Welcome:** Yes

## What We Don't Know

Do merchants prefer to hold BCH accumulated from validations, or immediately convert to fiat? What's the mix?

## Why It Matters

Merchant BCH preference affects:
- Local BCH buyer demand
- Liquidity design for Phase 1
- Merchant value proposition
- System circular flow

## Current Hypothesis

Merchants will be mixed: 30-40% hold BCH, 60-70% convert to fiat regularly, driven by individual risk tolerance and cash flow needs.

## Investigation Method

1. Survey merchants about BCH holding preferences
2. Interview Venezuelan shop owners about savings strategies
3. Model impact of various hold/convert ratios
4. Track actual behavior in Phase 0

## Success Criterion

Understanding of merchant BCH preferences with measured data from Phase 0 trials.

## Phase 0 Trial Integration

Track merchant BCH accumulation vs conversion rates over time.

**Estimated effort:** 3-5 hours
