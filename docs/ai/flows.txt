==============================================
OVERVIEW
==============================================

Four participant flows:
1. SENDER - Creates covenant, funds via Bizum (5-10 min)
2. RECIPIENT - Claims at merchant, receives cash (2 min active)
3. MERCHANT - Facilitates payout, earns 0.5% BCH (3 min)
4. BCH SELLER - Posts collateral, automated bot (0 min manual)

Typical end-to-end: 10 min - 24h (recipient chooses timing)
Covenant execution: 30 seconds (at claim)

==============================================
1. SENDER FLOW
==============================================

GOAL: Send €100 to Venezuelan recipient
DURATION: 5-10 minutes | STEPS: 5

---

STEP 1: CREATE REQUEST
Input: Recipient phone (+58 412 1234567), Amount (€100), Destination (Venezuela)
Display: Rate (€1 = 42.5 VES), Total cost (€101), Breakdown (€0.50 seller + €0.50 merchant)
Action: "Create Covenant" → Generate 4-digit code: 7392

---

STEP 2: WAIT FOR BCH SELLER
Backend: BCH seller bot accepts, calculates collateral (0.107 BCH for €107)
Notification: "Pay €101 via Bizum to +34 600 123 456, concept: +58 412 1234567"

---

STEP 3: PAY VIA BIZUM
Instructions: Send €101 Bizum, include "+58 412 1234567" in concept field
Timeout: 5 minutes (or request cancelled)

---

STEP 4: COVENANT CREATED
Backend: BCH seller receives Bizum, posts 0.107 BCH to covenant
Display: "Covenant created! Code: 7392, Active for 24h"
Notifications: Recipient SMS + Sender confirmation

---

STEP 5: TRACK STATUS
Display: Bounty code, time remaining, merchant location
Updates: T+18h (6h warning), T+23h (1h warning), T+24h (timeout/refund)
Outcomes: ✅ Claimed | ⏱️ Timeout (refund) | ⚠️ Margin call

==============================================
2. RECIPIENT FLOW
==============================================

GOAL: Receive €100 cash equivalent (4,250 VES)
DURATION: 30 seconds - 24 hours | STEPS: 4

---

STEP 1: RECEIVE SMS
Content: "€100 ready at [Merchant]. Code: 7392. Claim in 24h."
Action: Note code, plan merchant visit

---

STEP 2: AT MERCHANT
Recipient: "Code 7392"
Merchant: Opens app, enters 7392, shows details
Merchant asks: "Phone +58 412 1234567?" → Recipient confirms

---

STEP 3: COVENANT EXECUTES (30 SECONDS)
Backend: Merchant + Recipient sign covenant → Executes on-chain
Merchant receives: 0.098 BCH (~€102)
Display: "Payment received!"

---

STEP 4: RECEIVE CASH
Merchant hands: 4,250 VES
Recipient: (Optional) Signs receipt, leaves
SMS: "✅ Completed! You received 4,250 VES"

ERRORS:
- Wrong code → "Not found"
- Expired → "Refunded to sender"
- Already claimed → "Fraud detected"

==============================================
3. MERCHANT FLOW
==============================================

GOAL: Facilitate payout, earn 0.5% BCH fee
DURATION: 2-5 minutes | STEPS: 4

---

DASHBOARD:
Active covenants in area:
- #7392: €100 → 4,250 VES (22h left) - Fee: 21.25 VES (0.5%)
- #8451: €150 → 6,375 VES (18h left) - Fee: 31.88 VES (0.5%)

---

STEP 1: RECIPIENT ARRIVES
Action: "Claim Bounty" → Enter code 7392

---

STEP 2: VERIFY
Display: Recipient phone, Amount (4,250 VES), Merchant receives (0.098 BCH)
Verify: Ask for phone/ID confirmation
Action: "Confirm Payout" (or "Cancel" if fraud)

---

STEP 3: SIGN COVENANT
Backend: Generate signatures, execute covenant (30 seconds)
Display: "Payment received! 0.098 BCH (~€102)"

---

STEP 4: HAND CASH
Action: Count 4,250 VES, hand to recipient
App: "Cash handed" (final confirmation)
Complete: Transaction logged, BCH received

MERCHANT STATS:
- Today: 12 covenants, 0.58 BCH earned (~€600)
- Average: 3 min/transaction, 96% success rate

==============================================
4. BCH SELLER FLOW (AUTOMATED BOT)
==============================================

GOAL: Post collateral, earn 0.5% + hedge
DURATION: Automated | MANUAL: Margin calls only

---

BOT DASHBOARD:
- Inventory: 2.5 BCH available
- Active: 8 covenants (0.856 BCH locked)
- Today: €4.50 fees + hedge gains

---

STEP 1: NEW REQUEST (AUTOMATIC)
Receive: €100 covenant request, +58 412 1234567
Check: Inventory > 0.107 BCH? Yes → Accept
Wait: Sender Bizum payment (5 min timeout)

---

STEP 2: RECEIVE BIZUM (AUTOMATIC)
SMS: "Received €101, concept: +58 412 1234567"
Parse: Match phone to request
Action: Post 0.107 BCH to covenant, activate

---

STEP 3: MONITOR (AUTOMATIC)
Track: BCH price every 30s
Alert: If BCH drops >7% → Margin call
Normal: Wait for claim or timeout (24h)

---

STEP 4: MARGIN CALL (MANUAL, 5% cases)
SMS: "⚠️ Margin call! #7392, BCH -8%, respond in 60 min"
Options:
  A) Add 0.010 BCH (restore buffer) → Covenant continues
  B) Do nothing → Split refund (€100.50 → sender, €6.40 → seller)

---

STEP 5: COMPLETE (AUTOMATIC)
SUCCESS (95%): Covenant executes → Seller gets €100.50 + 0.009 BCH residual
TIMEOUT (5%): Split refund → Seller keeps €100.50 from Bizum

ECONOMICS (per €100 transaction):
- Fee: €0.50 fixed
- Hedge: ~€0.50 average (BCH volatility gains)
- Total: ~€1.00 / €7 locked / 12h avg = ~14% daily ROI

==============================================
ERROR SCENARIOS
==============================================

SENDER:
- Bizum timeout (5 min) → Request cancelled, retry
- Wrong concept field → Bot can't match, manual resolution
- Insufficient balance → Bank rejects, retry

RECIPIENT:
- Wrong code → "Not found", check SMS
- Expired (>24h) → "Refunded to sender"
- Already claimed → "Fraud detected", contact support

MERCHANT:
- Verification fails → Reject claim (protect network)
- No cash on hand → Recipient tries different merchant
- App crash → Covenant executes anyway (on-chain), refresh

BCH SELLER:
- Margin call missed → Split refund auto-executes
- Bot offline → SMS fallback alerts
- No inventory → Reject requests, refill

==============================================
END-TO-END TIMING
==============================================

ACTIVE TIME (human involvement):
- Sender: 5-10 min (Bizum payment)
- Recipient: 2 min (at merchant)
- Merchant: 3 min (verify + payout)
- BCH seller: 0 min (automated)

WAITING TIME (passive):
- Covenant creation: ~1 min (bot processes Bizum)
- Recipient decides when to claim: 0h - 24h
- Covenant execution: 30 seconds (on-chain)

TOTAL END-TO-END: 10 min - 24h (mostly recipient timing choice)

==============================================
PHASE 0 SUCCESS METRICS
==============================================

TARGET RATES:
- Execution rate: >95% (covenants claimed)
- Timeout rate: <5% (unclaimed after 24h)
- Margin call rate: <10% (BCH drops >7%)
- Dispute rate: 0% (trusted parties)

MEASUREMENT:
- Time-to-claim distribution (0-3h, 3-6h, 6-12h, 12-24h, >24h)
- Margin call frequency and response rate
- User satisfaction surveys (sender, recipient, merchant)
- Error rates by type

ADJUSTMENTS:
- If 80% claim <6h: Reduce timeout to 12h, test 5% overcollateralization
- If margin calls >15%: Increase to 10% overcollateralization
- If disputes occur: Add social media transparency layer

==============================================
COVENANT STATE MACHINE
==============================================

States:
1. PENDING_BIZUM: Request created, waiting for BCH seller
2. AWAITING_PAYMENT: BCH seller accepted, waiting for sender Bizum
3. ACTIVE: Covenant on-chain, recipient can claim (24h window)
4. MARGIN_CALL: BCH dropped >7%, waiting for seller response (60 min)
5. EXECUTING: Both parties signed, covenant executing (30 sec)
6. COMPLETED: Covenant executed, cash handed
7. TIMEOUT_REFUND: 24h expired, split refund processed
8. MARGIN_REFUND: Margin call unanswered, split refund processed
9. CANCELLED: Sender Bizum timeout, request cancelled

Transitions:
- 1→2: BCH seller accepts
- 2→3: Sender pays Bizum, seller posts collateral
- 3→4: BCH drops >7%
- 3→5: Recipient arrives, both sign
- 4→3: Seller adds BCH (margin call resolved)
- 4→8: 60 min timeout (no seller response)
- 5→6: Covenant executes, merchant hands cash
- 3→7: 24h timeout (no claim)
- 2→9: 5 min Bizum timeout
