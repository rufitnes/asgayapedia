WHAT IS ASGAYA?
==============================================

Asgaya is a permissionless, peer-to-peer remittance protocol that enables cross-border money
transfers using Bitcoin Cash as the settlement layer, with fiat on/off ramps through local
payment systems.

**Tagline:** Bitcoin Cash remittances with <1% fees, no KYC, self-custody

**Key Innovation:** Two-step settlement with pull-based BCH purchases eliminates volatility risk


CORE REQUIREMENTS
------------------

Asgaya is designed to satisfy three fundamental requirements:

1. CHEAPER THAN LEGACY (<1% fees)
   - Traditional remittances: 6.49% average cost
   - Asgaya target: <1% through market-rate exchanges + free P2P rails
   - On €100 transfer: Save €5.49

2. PROMOTE MERCHANT ADOPTION
   - Every transaction creates economic incentives for participants
   - Merchants earn ~0.247% per transaction
   - Liquidity Providers earn ~0.247% per settlement
   - Self-sustaining network growth

3. PERMISSIONLESS
   - No KYC requirements
   - Minimal hardware (Android phone)
   - Minimal knowledge (if you can use WhatsApp, you can use Asgaya)
   - Self-custody (users control their BCH)


FEE STRUCTURE
-------------

Total fee: 1% of transfer amount

On a €100 transfer, the 1% (€1.00) is distributed as:
- Exchange fee (Kraken): ~€0.26 (0.26%)
- Escrow operator: ~€0.247 (0.247%)
- Merchant: ~€0.247 (0.247%)
- Liquidity Provider: ~€0.247 (0.247%) - if instant settlement selected

How it works: The 1% fee first covers the Kraken exchange cost (~0.26%), then the
remaining ~0.74% is split equally three ways among the participants.

Comparison: €1.00 (Asgaya) vs €6.49 (traditional) = 6.5× cheaper

THE PROBLEM
==============================================

REMITTANCE CRISIS
-----------------

Global remittances: $685 billion annually
Average cost: 6.49%
Money lost to fees: $44.5 billion per year

Despite UN and G20 pledges to reduce costs to 3%, the reality is stark:
over $44.5 billion never reaches its destination.


WHY ARE REMITTANCES EXPENSIVE?
-------------------------------

1. WALLED GARDENS
   - Payment systems don't interoperate (Bizum can't send to PagoMóvil)
   - Each system has monopoly within its jurisdiction
   - No competition = high fees

2. BUREAUCRACY
   - KYC/AML compliance costs
   - Multiple intermediaries
   - Regulatory overhead in each jurisdiction

3. VOLATILITY RISK
   - Previous crypto remittance attempts failed due to volatility
   - Price changes between send and receive destroyed value
   - Users lost trust after bad experiences

4. BOOTSTRAPPING PROBLEM
   - Merchants won't join without users
   - Users won't join without merchants
   - Chicken-and-egg deadlock


PREVIOUS CRYPTO ATTEMPTS (AND WHY THEY FAILED)
-----------------------------------------------

Bitcoin, Ethereum, Stellar, Ripple, etc. all tried crypto remittances.
They all failed for the same reason: VOLATILITY.

Scenario:
- Day 1: Sender sends €100, buys 0.01 BTC
- Day 5: Price drops 10%
- Day 5: Recipient cashes out, gets €90

Result: Recipient lost €10 due to volatility, not fees.

**Critical insight:** Volatility protection isn't optional, it's foundational.

==============================================
THE SOLUTION
==============================================

ASGAYA'S APPROACH
-----------------

Asgaya solves the four problems:

1. WALLED GARDENS → Use BCH as bridge
   - Bizum (Spain) → BCH → PagoMóvil (Venezuela)
   - Free P2P rails on both sides
   - BCH handles cross-border settlement

2. BUREAUCRACY → Permissionless design
   - No KYC (use existing payment apps)
   - No intermediaries (direct peer-to-peer)
   - No special licenses needed

3. VOLATILITY → Two-step settlement with pull system
   - EUR held until recipient claims (0% volatility)
   - BCH purchase only when ready to settle (30-second window)
   - Volatility risk reduced from 20 minutes to 30 seconds

4. BOOTSTRAPPING → Economic incentives
   - Merchants earn €0.247 per €100 transaction
   - Build network by mobilizing lost fees
   - Every transaction creates a new merchant advocate


TWO-STEP SETTLEMENT (THE KEY INNOVATION)
-----------------------------------------

Traditional crypto remittance:
1. Sender buys crypto
2. Crypto sits (volatility risk!)
3. Recipient sells crypto
4. Total volatility window: Hours or days

Asgaya's approach:
1. Sender sends EUR to escrow (0% volatility - it's fiat!)
2. EUR sits in escrow (no BCH bought yet)
3. Recipient goes to merchant, ready to claim
4. Escrow buys BCH (30-second transaction)
5. BCH settles to merchant
6. Merchant gives cash to recipient
7. Total BCH volatility window: 30 seconds

Result: 99.5% reduction in volatility exposure


PULL SYSTEM
-----------

Key principle: Don't buy BCH until someone is ready to receive fiat.

Push system (traditional):
- Sender triggers BCH purchase
- BCH sits waiting for recipient
- Volatility risk: High

Pull system (Asgaya):
- Recipient triggers BCH purchase (by claiming at merchant)
- BCH purchased and settled immediately
- Volatility risk: Minimal

Analogy: Don't make the sandwich until the customer walks in the door.

HOW IT WORKS (STEP-BY-STEP)
==============================================

PARTICIPANTS
------------

1. SENDER (María, Barcelona)
   - Has EUR in Spanish bank account
   - Has Bizum app on phone
   - Wants to send €100 to Venezuela

2. RECIPIENT (Elena, Caracas)
   - Has Venezuelan phone number
   - Needs to receive bolívares (VES)
   - Doesn't need bank account or special app

3. MERCHANT (Pulpería owner, Caracas)
   - Has Android phone
   - Has Asgaya app installed
   - Has cash available (bolívares)
   - Earns ~€0.247 per transaction

4. LIQUIDITY PROVIDER (Optional)
   - Has EUR and BCH
   - Provides instant settlement
   - Earns ~€0.247 per settlement

5. ESCROW OPERATOR
   - Holds EUR temporarily
   - Coordinates BCH purchase
   - Earns ~€0.247 per transaction


THE FLOW (EUR → VES REMITTANCE)
--------------------------------

STEP 1: SENDER INITIATES (María in Barcelona)
- Opens Asgaya app
- Enters: €100 to send
- Enters: Elena's Venezuelan phone (0412-XXX-5678)
- Enters: Recipient name (Elena)
- App calculates: 4,999,500 VES will arrive (using live rates)
- App shows fee: €1.00 total
- María approves

STEP 2: SENDER PAYS ESCROW (via Bizum)
- App generates unique code: ASG-7X2K9
- María opens Bizum app
- Sends €100 to escrow's Bizum account
- Concept field: "ASG-7X2K9 Elena 0412-XXX-5678"
- Payment completes instantly (Bizum is free and instant in Spain)

STEP 3: ESCROW RECEIVES & HOLDS
- Escrow's NotificationListener detects Bizum payment
- Parses concept field, extracts code + recipient phone
- Matches payment to María's pending transaction
- EUR sits in escrow (NO BCH PURCHASED YET - this is key!)
- Sends SMS to Elena: "María sent you 4,999,500 VES. Code: ASG-7X2K9. Go to merchant."

STEP 4: RECIPIENT GOES TO MERCHANT (Elena in Caracas)
- Elena receives SMS notification
- Goes to nearby pulpería (small neighborhood store)
- Merchant has Asgaya app installed

STEP 5: MERCHANT ENTERS CODE
- Merchant opens Asgaya app
- Enters code: ASG-7X2K9
- App shows: "Elena, 4,999,500 VES"
- Merchant confirms details match

STEP 6: CASH-OUT (THE CRITICAL MOMENT)
- Merchant gives Elena 4,999,500 VES in cash
- Elena confirms receipt in merchant's app (face-to-face)
- Merchant confirms delivery in app

STEP 7: PULL SYSTEM TRIGGERS (BCH Settlement)
- ONLY NOW does escrow buy BCH (pull-based!)
- Escrow sends Bizum to Kraken: €99.74 (€100 - €0.26 Kraken fee)
- Kraken instantly converts to BCH at market rate
- BCH sent to merchant's wallet
- Merchant receives: ~€0.247 in BCH (their fee)
- LP receives: ~€0.247 in BCH (if instant settlement)
- Escrow keeps: ~€0.247 (their fee)

Total BCH volatility window: ~30 seconds (Kraken exchange + BCH transaction)

STEP 8: COMPLETION
- María sees: "Delivered" status
- Elena has: Cash in hand
- Merchant has: BCH reward + new customer
- System has: New trusted merchant node


WHAT IF RECIPIENT DOESN'T CLAIM?
---------------------------------

Unclaimed transaction policy:
- Claim window: 24 hours
- Notifications: 12h (reminder), 18h (sender alert), 23h (urgent)
- If unclaimed: Auto-refund sender
- Refund amount: €99.90 (€100 - €0.10 processing fee)
- Processing fee covers: Manual refund work + 24h liquidity lock

Note: No BCH was bought (pull system!), so no exchange fees incurred.


WHAT IF THERE'S A DISPUTE?
---------------------------

Merchant says: "I gave cash"
Recipient says: "I never received it"

Resolution (MVP):
- Escrow investigates (24h deadline)
- Evidence requested: Video, photos, witnesses, GPS
- Default: Favor merchant (they're scarce resource)
- 3-strike system for dishonest merchants
- Future: Community arbitration, dispute pool

Safe confirmation sequence (prevents most disputes):
1. Recipient shows code to merchant
2. Merchant enters code (validates)
3. Merchant hands cash to recipient
4. Merchant confirms "I gave cash" (in recipient's presence)
5. Recipient confirms "I received cash" (in merchant's presence)
6. Both confirmations done face-to-face

Self-auditing through UX design!

KEY FEATURES
==============================================

VOLATILITY PROTECTION
----------------------

Problem: Crypto price changes destroy value
Solution: Two-step settlement + pull system

Volatility window:
- Traditional crypto remittance: Hours to days
- Asgaya: 30 seconds (BCH settlement only)

Result: 99.5%+ reduction in volatility exposure


PERMISSIONLESS ACCESS
----------------------

No KYC required because:
- Users already KYC'd by their banks (Bizum, PagoMóvil)
- We're just coordinating existing payment systems
- No new accounts, no new identity verification

Anyone can participate:
- Senders: Just need Bizum account (already have it)
- Recipients: Just need phone number (already have it)
- Merchants: Just need Android phone + Asgaya app


COMPETITIVE PRICING
-------------------

Traditional remittance: 6.49% average
Asgaya: 1% total

Why so cheap?
1. Free P2P rails (Bizum, PagoMóvil - no fees)
2. Market-rate exchanges (Kraken - 0.26%, not 6.49%)
3. No intermediaries (direct peer-to-peer)
4. No regulatory overhead (permissionless)

Fee breakdown on €100:
- Kraken exchange: €0.26 (unavoidable)
- Participants (escrow, merchant, LP): €0.74 (split 3 ways)
- Total: €1.00 (vs €6.49 traditional)


MERCHANT INCENTIVES
-------------------

Why merchants join:
1. Earn ~€0.247 per €100 transaction (0.247% fee)
2. Attract new customers (recipients come to their store)
3. Drive repeat business (recipients become regular customers)
4. No risk (receive BCH instantly, can hold or sell)

Example: 100 transactions/month = €24.70 extra income
In Venezuela (or Honduras, Nicaragua, etc.), this is significant!


SELF-CUSTODY
------------

Users control their private keys:
- Merchants receive BCH directly to their wallet
- No custodial risk (no exchange can freeze funds)
- True peer-to-peer transfer
- Bitcoin Cash ethos maintained


BCH AS SETTLEMENT LAYER
------------------------

Why Bitcoin Cash (not Bitcoin, Ethereum, etc.)?

1. Low fees: ~€0.01 per transaction (vs €5-50 for Bitcoin)
2. Fast confirmation: 10 minutes average, 0-conf reliable
3. Reliable: No congestion, predictable fees
4. Philosophy: Designed for payments (not just store of value)

BCH is the bridge, not the destination. Users never need to think about it.
