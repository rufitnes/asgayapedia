# Asgaya Documentation

> Bitcoin Cash remittances with <1% fees, no KYC, self-custody

## What is Asgaya?

Asgaya is a permissionless remittance protocol that enables cross-border money transfers using Bitcoin Cash as settlement layer, with fiat on/off ramps through local payment systems (Bizum in Spain, PagoMóvil in Venezuela).

**Key Innovation:** Two-step settlement with pull-based BCH purchases eliminates volatility risk.
